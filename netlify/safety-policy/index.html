Staff & Women Safety Policy

Royal Venue records safety requirements for eligible late-night catering and hospitality shifts. Such shifts require an assigned escort vehicle, identified driver and certified bodyguard or bouncer, and a verified safe-drop timestamp before the shift can be completed in our operations system. Staff may report a safety concern to the duty manager at any time. Home drop details are restricted to authorized admins and the assigned bodyguard, and each recorded access is logged.

For privacy or grievance requests, email privacy@royalvenue.in. This is an operational template; the venue should validate its retention periods, grievance contact and actual processing vendors before deployment.

Reference: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.
