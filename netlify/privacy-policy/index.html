Privacy Policy

Information we collect: account name, email, phone and password hash; enquiry and booking event type, dates, guest count and notes; WhatsApp support messages and attachments; vendor business and contact details; and, for staff safety operations, staff contact, shift, driver, vehicle and drop-location details. Passwords are stored using Django's password hashing system. Why we use it: to answer enquiries, check availability, manage bookings and payments, arrange vendor and staff services, operate customer support, protect people and prevent fraud, maintain records required for legal or accounting purposes, and meet legal obligations. Marketing messages are optional and require a separate affirmative choice. Sharing and safeguards: data is available only to personnel who need it for their role. Booking details may be shared with selected venues, vendors or staff to deliver requested services. When configured, WhatsApp Cloud API and payment, hosting or storage providers process the data needed to provide those services. Staff home-drop information is restricted to authorized operations admins and the assigned bodyguard; access is logged. We use access controls, secure password hashing, CSRF protection and upload type/size checks. Retention and your choices: we retain information while needed to provide the service, resolve issues, protect safety, and satisfy applicable legal and accounting requirements, then delete or de-identify it when appropriate. You may request access, correction, erasure, grievance redressal or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent by emailing privacy@royalvenue.in. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out or processing required by law. We will verify the requester before acting. This notice applies to Royal Venue's website and booking operations. For questions or grievances, contact privacy@royalvenue.in. Replace this address with the monitored Data Protection contact before launch.

For privacy or grievance requests, email privacy@royalvenue.in. This is an operational template; the venue should validate its retention periods, grievance contact and actual processing vendors before deployment.

Reference: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.
